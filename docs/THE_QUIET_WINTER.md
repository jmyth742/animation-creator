# The Quiet Winter — a feature episode in six chapters (~20 min)

Same two characters. A different story: not the myth's beats, but the years
between them — the part no telling of the legend bothers with.

**The premise.** Snow falls in the land of eternal summer, for the first
time in anyone's forever. Niamh knows what nobody says aloud: the land
listens to the hearts it keeps, and the snow is Oisín's homesickness made
weather. She has to decide whether to tell him — because a keeper who
manages her guest's heart is a gaoler, and she swore she'd never be that.

**Why this story fits this machine.** Two characters only. Landscape as the
protagonist's inner life — the most Cartoon Saloon idea available to us.
An entire near-wordless storm chapter (the Samurai Jack move) that finally
spends the storm_cliffs and stormy_sea plates. Stillness as register;
motion rationed to where the weather breaks. And winter gives every
location a second face — new plates, same geography, so 20 minutes never
runs out of picture without leaving home.

## The six chapters (each ~3:20, rendered as ep18–ep23, assembled as one)

1. **The First Cold** — a single flake over the lake at golden hour.
   Wonder, play, neither has seen snow *here*. Ends on Niamh alone,
   recognising it, saying nothing.
2. **What the Land Hears** — she consults the place itself; the falls
   thin to ice-glass. He walks the white valley delighted. Her close:
   the snow is him.
3. **The Argument They Don't Have** — the conversation engine at full
   stretch: shot/reverse over long holds, she probes without telling,
   he half-lies about being content. First storm on the horizon.
4. **The Storm** — near-wordless. The weather breaks out of the valley;
   the sea turns. Every motion shot the episode owns is spent here.
5. **The Telling** — she says what the snow is, and her terms: she will
   not keep a man whose heart makes winter. His confession — the
   homesickness said aloud, the longest held close in the series.
6. **Thaw** — the melt, the valley returned. One patch of snow is kept,
   on purpose: *so we remember it was let in, not locked out.* Closing
   wides mirror chapter one's.

## Direction notes (from the lineage, applied)

- Ch1/Ch6 rhyme shot-for-shot where possible (Kaguya's symmetry).
- Ch4 target: ≤2 lines total. Sound design carries it.
- The confession in Ch5 is ONE unbroken close, 15s ceiling, written to it.
- Winter plates are the same compositions as the summer ones where they
  exist — the viewer should feel the same place changed, not a new place.

## Production

Each chapter is written, linted, preflighted and rendered as its own
episode; the 20-minute cut is an assembly with chapter cards. GPU budget
~50–60h total. Winter plate set forged first (gen__winter_*).
